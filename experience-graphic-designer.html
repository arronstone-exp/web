Graphic
Designer

@ UPDESCO

During the critical COVID-19 pandemic period, I worked with UPDESCO (Uttar Pradesh Development Systems Corporation) as a Graphic Designer, contributing to public health awareness campaigns across Uttar Pradesh, India. This role involved creating impactful visual communications to educate and inform citizens during unprecedented times.

Key Responsibilities

Banner & Poster Design

Designed high-impact banners and posters for display across major cities in Uttar Pradesh, India, ensuring clear communication of COVID-19 safety guidelines and health protocols.

Video Editor Collaboration

Worked closely with video editors to create cohesive multimedia campaigns, providing graphics, overlays, and visual elements to spread awareness and safety guidelines.

Government Communication

Created official government communications following strict brand guidelines while ensuring accessibility and clarity for diverse audiences across urban and rural areas.

Team Collaboration

Collaborating with team members including content writers, photographers, and officials to ensure project success and timely delivery of awareness materials.

Impact & Achievements

Citywide Campaign Reach

Designed over 50+ unique banner and poster variations deployed across major cities including Lucknow, Kanpur, Agra, Varanasi, and more, reaching millions of citizens with critical health information.

Rapid Turnaround

Maintained exceptional efficiency during crisis period, delivering high-quality designs within tight 24-48 hour deadlines to address evolving pandemic situations and new guidelines.

Public Health Impact

Contributed to statewide public awareness efforts that helped communicate essential safety protocols including mask-wearing, social distancing, hand hygiene, and vaccination information to diverse demographics.

Multilingual Design

Created designs in multiple languages (Hindi, English, and regional dialects) to ensure accessibility and comprehension across all linguistic communities in Uttar Pradesh.
